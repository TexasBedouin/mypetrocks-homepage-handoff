# Add your pet's stickers to WhatsApp

It takes about a minute. WhatsApp has a built-in sticker maker, and it removes the white background for you.

## On your phone

1. **Save the stickers.** Long-press each sticker on your downloads page and choose "Save to Photos", or tap "Download sticker sheet".
2. **Open WhatsApp and any chat.** Your own "message yourself" chat is a good place to test.
3. **Open the sticker panel.** Tap the sticker or emoji icon next to the message box, then tap **+** or **Create**.
4. **Pick a sticker from your photos.** WhatsApp cuts out the background automatically.
5. **Tap Send or Save.** The sticker is now in your sticker tray. Repeat for the other 8.
6. **Send your pet to everyone.** Your stickers work in every chat, forever.

## On a computer (WhatsApp Web or Desktop)

Open a chat, click the sticker icon, click **Create**, and upload a sticker file. Doing it on a computer is quickest if you downloaded all 9 at once.

## No "Create" button?

Your WhatsApp is older, or the feature hasn't reached your phone yet. Try these in order:

1. Update WhatsApp from the App Store or Google Play, then look again.
2. Or install a free sticker app like **Sticker.ly**. Make a new pack, add your saved stickers, then tap "Add to WhatsApp".
